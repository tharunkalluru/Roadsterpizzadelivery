## 1. Actual observations and current limit

The public page opened in a remote Chrome browser on 17 October 2026. Its title identified Whiteout and escaping a collapsing city. The opening screen had a near-black background, large condensed uppercase white WHITEOUT lettering, generous spacing, a short subtitle, thin dividers, muted keyboard-control blocks, and Start/Settings controls. Start remained in its disabled loading state.

The browser console reported a THREE.WebGLRenderer failure to create a graphics context, with its graphics renderer disabled. This establishes that the observed page attempts to use Three.js. It does not establish how gameplay looks or performs.

**Gameplay, car modeling, streets, materials, lighting, weather, camera motion, effects, frame rate, and phone performance were not observed in this preparation environment.** No guessed description of those properties should be presented as an inspection result. No Whiteout screenshots, code, soundtrack, or other assets are redistributed in this kit.
