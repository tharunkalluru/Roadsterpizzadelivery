- One entire delivery contract has one shared flight allowance: 25 seconds by default, with stated 20/30-second alternatives. Continuous and split flights are allowed. Takeoff, hover, travel, air braking, coasting, and descent consume the same bank. Landing reserve is inside it. No ground recharge, fixed per-launch duration cap, or free emergency flight.
- Keep resource accounting, launch abort, qualified touchdown, recovery, pause, and checkpoint rules exactly consistent with Section 12 and `config/flight-budget.json`.
- The requested map remains selected connected roads beside all three operating Hyderabad Metro corridors, with justified real deviations. Uppal to Tarnaka and the first authentic slice are internal milestones.
- Preserve road scale, directions, medians, valid turns, levels, names, frontage order, and destination access. Imported OSM properties are source claims requiring the specified validation.
- Track `verified`, `provisional`, `missing`, and `scenario_overlay` per property. Unknown data cannot become a fabricated real shop, connector, height, or clearance.
- Require a real delivery loop with useful driving, readable traffic, flight, landing, valid stopping, results, failure, recovery, and retry.
- Target the user-selected Whiteout level of finish on authentic Hyderabad roads, with sustained mobile-browser play on Android and iOS. Native-like touch behavior does not establish installed packaging or store readiness.
