# Traffic continuity across sampled road profiles

The road audit exposed a separate movement defect: turn planning treated the last or first height sample as the whole physical approach. An actual west Tarnaka connection had a1.36m last sample and a0.141m first sample despite long usable approaches. Refining an unchanged road with collinear points could strand buses, cars and autos. Nearly parallel tangent intersections also made some ordinary-car continuation curves unstable.

`traffic-approach.ts` measures and caches the near-straight physical room, stops at real bends and caps work/room at60m. A bounded two-metre reference handles tiny endpoint chords caused by coordinate rounding while retaining a real ten-centimetre right-angle corner. Junction and in-edge planning share this room; almost-parallel lane continuations use the stable cubic already used for buses. Existing full-body, road-support, obstacle, radius and collision checks remain. Traffic clocks, frame cadence, density and ordinary camera code are unchanged.

Conservative single through-lane policy is honoured only for one-way modeled corridors; opposite-direction traffic, construction lanes and controlled expressway lanes keep their existing priority. Physical capacity intervals remain a separate road-geometry responsibility.

Root's focused checks pass6/6 and legacy traffic checks pass16/16. The earlier bus-service18-group and execution7-group checks passed during this change; they will run again in the final release check after road geometry is held. Independent final approach review passes7 groups: actual/coarse/dense west source geometry, all11,270 visible bus vertices including brake/reverse-light geometry over63 emitted asphalt/soffit samples, real Traffic movement onto the outgoing edge, and9 bus/car/auto full-approach executions under refinement. Real sharp-bend and blocked-median controls still reject unsafe motion. The frozen source fixture and [independent evidence](main-expansion-traffic-approach-independent.md) make this reproducible without `/tmp` files.

This closes the bounded sampling/turn-planning defect. It is not approval of the wider road-height/XY repair, native whole-game smoothness, or physical-phone FPS. Final geometry changes and native driving still need their own checks.
