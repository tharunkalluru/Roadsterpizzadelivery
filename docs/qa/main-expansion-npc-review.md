# Independent NPC candidate review — 2026-11-02

Reviewer: traffic_vehicle_realism (not the NPC producer). Verdict: **FAIL — do not release this appearance candidate yet.**

Inspected actual Chrome captures supplied by Root: `evidence/main-expansion/npc-front-candidate.png`, `npc-faces-candidate.png`, `npc-seated-candidate.png`, `npc-profile-candidate.png`. This is visual evidence, not an assessment inferred from tests or triangle counts.

## Blocking observations and producer feedback

1. **Disconnected anatomy:** upper arms are detached from the torso/shoulders; elbows and knees show large open seams. The profile view exposes a gap through the waist and the seated figures have floating upper arms. Join/intersect anatomically shaped shoulder and joint surfaces throughout the gait, rather than hiding cracks in the front view. Recheck full walk cycle from front, side and three-quarter views.
2. **Clothing reads as cardboard:** sari, salwar/dupatta, elder clothing and burqa fronts contain broad planar polygons, abrupt rectangular borders and detached flat strips. The sari's upper cloth is a giant skewed rectangle. Wrap clothing around the chest/shoulder/hip volumes with thickness, neckline and curved silhouette; remove detached rectangles and reduce literal floating stripe geometry. The burqa profile is visibly open behind the face/shoulder panel.
3. **Faces are not yet plausible people:** all four close heads have very similar masculine brow/nose/mouth/jaw, including the nominal female sample. The mouth/lower face appears open, dark and pinched; the eyes show a fixed wide stare with harsh bright sclera and dark points. Use a neutral closed mouth with continuous lip/chin surfaces, proportionate recessed eyes/lids and distinct age/gender/individual head proportions. Do not rely on a hairstyle or palette swap to establish facial diversity.
4. **Age/sex diversity is not fully proven:** child size and an adult/elder height contrast are visible, but teenagers look like smaller adult versions and female faces remain too similar to the broad adult male. Require recognizable adolescent shoulder/jaw/build proportions, female and elderly facial silhouette changes, and modest posture changes without caricature.
5. **Seated contact is unproven and visibly risky:** the standalone seated capture has no actual bike, handlebar, seat, floor or steering wheel. It cannot establish hand/foot/hip contact. The detached arms also persist in that view. The next review must show the exact production occupants installed in actual vehicles from side/front/three-quarter views, with hands meeting controls, feet supported and no heads through roofs.

## Positive changes retained for the next iteration

The head is volumetric, body silhouettes vary, fingers are modeled and the profile shows articulated walking. These are useful foundations but do not meet the requested believable 3D-person quality yet. Geometry/resource checks remain necessary after correcting the visible defects; they do not substitute for this review.

## Re-review evidence required

- Fresh production front, profile, three-quarter and face captures after correction.
- Two or more actual gait phases with joints connected.
- Real production vehicle occupants in their seats/at their handlebars, plus station/bus pedestrians.
- Day and night gameplay-distance captures with no body/cloth detachment.
- NPC producer must not self-approve; send exact revised sources/captures back to an independent reviewer.
