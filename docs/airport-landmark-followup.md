# Airport, bridge and elevated landmark follow-up

Reviewed 2026-10-26. These are local application changes; no publication or deployment is claimed by this record.

## Airport evidence and implementation

- [AAI RGIA aerodrome chart, 16 April 2026](https://aim-india.aai.aero/eaip/eaip-v2-04-2026/eAIP/VOHS-ADC.pdf): physical endpoints and dimensions for 09R/27L (4,260 × 60 m) and 09L/27R (3,707 × 45 m). The chart separates proposed infrastructure. An AAI safeguarding/NOCAS table lists conflicting future runway dimensions; these were not treated as operational infrastructure.
- [AAI Begumpet aerodrome chart, 2 October 2025](https://aim-india.aai.aero/eaip-v2-05-2025/eAIP/VOHY-ADC.pdf), [current AIP September 2026 edition](https://aim-india.aai.aero/eaip/eaip-v2-09-2026/eAIP/IN-AD%202.1VOHY-en-GB.html), and [AD2.12 physical endpoints](https://aim-india.aai.aero/eAIP_Archive/08-08-2024/eAIP/IN-AD%202.1VOHY-en-GB.html): runway 09/27 is 3,230 × 45 m; displaced thresholds are 479 m and 738 m. The code uses physical runway ends, draws threshold markings inside those ends, and preserves the true approximately 88.9° axis.
- [RGIA operator master plan](https://www.hyderabad.aero/about-us/master-plan): main runway 4,260 m, dependent secondary/standby runway, modular passenger terminal and associated apron/cargo/MRO facilities. No third operational runway was invented.
- Official OpenStreetMap API snapshots, retrieved 2026-10-26: [Begumpet bounds](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=78.445,17.447,78.489,17.459), [RGIA bounds](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=78.405,17.223,78.457,17.247). OSM contributors, ODbL 1.0. The compact source snapshot contains 89 Begumpet and 183 RGIA taxiway/apron/terminal/hangar features with way IDs and latitude/longitude. Existing game/shopfront source files were retained.

The application now draws the actual mapped taxiway, apron and terminal outlines, runways, centre lines, edge lines, displaced threshold bars, designators and edge lights. Runway pieces are owned by local 1 km tiles; they do not disappear because a terminal several kilometres away unloads. Nine runway tiles intersect the three runways and all nine already exist in the playable source index. Procedural trees/crops and replacement buildings are excluded from mapped airport pavement and AAI runway strips. Terminal catalogue anchors now follow the mapped terminal polygons: inherited Begumpet was on the runway; inherited RGIA was approximately 400 m from its mapped passenger terminal.

Generic unbranded aircraft share one geometry/material. Each airport has two parked aircraft at low quality or up to four at normal quality, plus at most one moving departure. Parked positions are generated inside mapped apron polygons; these are illustrative parking positions, not verified stand assignments. Moving aircraft follow connected mapped taxiway paths to a valid main-runway intersection, then roll and climb. Disconnected taxiways are not joined across grass and the dependent secondary runway is not simultaneously animated as an independent active runway. Motion is illustrative, not an ATC/aviation simulation or current flight timetable.

Confidence: **high** for published runway lengths, width, threshold coordinates and designators; **medium** for OSM plan shapes; **provisional** for facade heights, materials, parking positions and operating animation. Game terrain is a flat local datum: published airport elevations above mean sea level are not incorrectly used as hundreds-of-metres-high platforms. Airport terminal architecture, hangar elevations, exact stand markings and every taxiway width still require further primary-photo/survey comparison.

## Durgam Cheruvu

- [L&T builder as-built release, 25 September 2020](https://www.larsentoubro.com/media/42542/2020-09-25-lt-built-iconic-durgam-cheruvu-cable-stay-bridge-dedicated-to-the-people-of-hyderbad.pdf): main span 233.85 m, cable-stayed portion 435 m including approaches, overall width 25.8 m, 52 stay cables and 1.8 m footpaths.
- [GHMC budget 2020–21, page 23](https://ghmc.gov.in/Budget/Budget-2020-21.pdf): main-span arrangement preserves the lake with pylons at its banks.
- [Designer Atelier VAMA project page](https://ateliervama.com/portfolio/durgam-cheravu-lake-bridge/): three lanes in each direction, single-plane cables, fish-belly girder and footpaths. The three primary designer images were inspected; they are **design visualisations, not as-built photographs**. They support the pointed leaf/spear head shape replacing the unrelated bulb in the old model, but do not prove all present-day decorative details.
- [Government-hosted technical study, page 60](https://pwdroads.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/pwd_lipl_in_oid_3/menu/document/binder_final_24-04-2025_compressed_compressed_compressed_0.pdf) reports 28 m upper pylons. Its older lane/deck-length values conflict with the builder's completion release, so the latter governs deck width and cable count. Pylon decorative dimensions remain stylised.
- In-repository OSM bridge polygon `w761604461` supplies centre and axis. The model centre/bearing now follows that rectangle instead of an approximate hand anchor. Lake water remains the mapped `w28131043` shoreline; invented concrete promenade slabs at the lake's centre were removed.

The deck is continuous beneath inherited road paint, with two footpaths, railings, two pylons 233.85 m apart and 52 structural stays. Thin coloured illumination follows each white cable rather than doubling every stay into detached coloured bars. The existing mapped road altitude (~6.8 game metres) is retained; an absolute as-built water/bed/deck survey was not inferred. Current primary OSM API evidence also confirms three lanes on six named bridge source ways. The five canonical bridge edges match 97.7–100% of samples within 5 m, with 95th-percentile differences below 0.1 m. The serialized importer updates every canonical and context copy to three lanes and a 9.6 m carriageway, preserving all source XY and unrelated shopfronts. The 3.2 m per-lane width is an explicit rendering assumption; inherited edge segmentation extends up to 34 m beyond exact source-way overlap at two endpoints. See `pipeline/evidence/durgam-lanes-audit-2026-10-26.json` for counts and matching evidence.

## Landing and parking on geometry

Landmark support is captured from emitted geometry using the same YXZ transforms as rendering. Upward-facing triangles are stored in streamed 30 m cells with a source landmark ID. Height queries interpolate each triangle and respect the maximum reachable height. Undersides/near-vertical faces do not become roofs; arch openings are not filled by a bounding rectangle, and rocky hills use their actual visible facets instead of four invented concentric flat terraces.

This replaces legacy landmark footprint roof heights for landing support. Existing broad side footprints remain a legacy approximation and are bypassed during actual top contact; full concave side/arch-wall collision still needs a geometry-volume pass. A successful support regression does not prove a completed human-style landing, drive or park at every landmark. Runtime visible acceptance is recorded separately by the parent task.

## Checks

`node tools/airport-landmark-regressions.mjs` validates official runway endpoints/widths, both mapped taxi routes, independent runway-tile support, displaced thresholds, finite/bounded geometry, sloped interpolation, real arch openings, aircraft limits and the documented bridge span/cable count. `node tools/landmark-regressions.mjs` retains catalogue/map/flag/model guards. See parent acceptance record for the final whole-application build and actual game interactions.

The bridge importer applied three lanes to five canonical edges and 17 pack/context copies across two packs. A full-data repeat found the same 17 copies and changed zero copies/packs, recorded in `pipeline/evidence/durgam-lanes-idempotence-2026-10-26.json`. Three importer unit tests cover evidence refusal, all-copy/null-name-context handling, exact XY/context and unrelated shop/building preservation, manifest sizes and byte-idempotence.

Remaining limitations: facade/photo detail, precise aircraft stand assignments, full airport ground handling, precise surveyed bridge lane widths and endpoint transitions, absolute terrain survey, concave landmark side collision and human-style land/ride/park acceptance across all landmarks. Source coverage is not street verification.
