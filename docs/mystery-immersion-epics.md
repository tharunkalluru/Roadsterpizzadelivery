### Defects found during acceptance

1. Normal opening spawn could face a construction barrier: require a real, non-work-zone main carriageway and a clear 40 m body path.
2. Large authored truck could clip a median despite a sufficient road width: validate the complete body along the actual route and prefer the clear central carriageway.
3. Hidden area notice reserved space and pushed guidance into the scene: reclaim the lane and use a 48 px strip (44 px on narrow screens).
4. Drop/Throw hint could overlap the map on a short desktop: place the complete action block using measured blockers, independent of input mode.
5. Compact title wrapped a third card and clipped Play: keep a short landscape row, with both 44 px actions visible.
6. Truck approach marker lay ahead of the permitted call region: anchor the gate to the actual rear door, retaining the marker behind the ramp.
7. Road routing could show a long detour within metres of an off-road moving target: use actual rear/ramp position and truck heading for final approach.
8. Separate ramp support tests missed the full car's front hitting the raised bed edge: add a real Player-driven moving-ramp integration test and narrowly join the connected ramp/floor contact.
9. Removing a content entry could reset a valid v2 completed history: preserve genuine known completed entries while rejecting malformed, duplicate or non-prefix histories.


### Compact title regression — isolated browser fixture

The short landscape title previously let the third instruction card wrap to a new row, putting Play below the viewport. `src/ui/title.ts` now holds the three cards in one row, pairs each icon with its heading, and retains all copy plus two 44 px actions. At heights below 340 px, heading/spacing condense further. The two-card legacy title retains two columns.

`tests/browser/title-layout.browser.ts` instantiates the production `Hud` and `TitleScreen`, uses the shipped Bungee/Baloo fonts and paint-safety CSS, and measures every title/card/button rectangle plus button-center hit testing. A separate mobile Chrome Playwright session passed 568×320, 667×375, 800×360, 852×393 and 918×384: all content inside the viewport, cards in one row, no vertical scrolling, both buttons 44 px high. Play/Settings bottoms were 277.1 px on the 320 px screen and 310.8 px on the 375 px screen. Screenshots: `output/playwright/title-568x320.png` and `output/playwright/title-667x375.png`. These are browser emulation results, not physical-phone evidence. Typecheck, mobile-ui 5/5 and diff check also passed. The isolated test browser and temporary server were closed afterward.

### Moving-ramp integration acceptance

`delivery-ramp-driving.test.ts` runs production `Player.update`, moving-truck module updates and `MovingRailInteraction` in their real order at 30 and 60 simulation updates per second. After initial spawn it uses throttle/brake input only. In both runs the truck continues at 6 m/s; the car rides the ramp, crosses onto the bed with zero collision nudges, reveals only after actual rear entry, settles at rest relative to the moving truck and completes the visible handoff exactly once. Negative cases keep a missing/closed or detached ramp, a car below the ramp, ordinary train bodies, the side walls, logs and cab solid. This is deterministic production-physics evidence; it is not a claim that those update rates were measured on a phone.
