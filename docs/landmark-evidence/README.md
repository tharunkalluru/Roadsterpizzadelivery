# Landmark map and symbol review — 26 October 2026

This batch adds a shared catalogue for all **191** existing modeled places. The catalogue and models share stable IDs and the same world coordinates; the six Qutb Shahi tombs have separate IDs. All 191 anchors are inside the playable bounds. 190 anchors have source tiles; Osman Sagar's recorded anchor has no source tile. Its original coordinates are retained, and the nearest indexed tile owns rendering so a missing lake tile no longer silently omits the model. This does not establish that its inherited reservoir anchor is the correct dam or entrance location.

The map offers all 191 places through searchable, categorized results. Cached silhouettes include Charminar, forts, domes, towers, bridge, statue and other architectural/category icons. Viewport culling, zoom clusters with counts and limited labels reduce clutter. Search results remain available even when a place is clustered or off screen. Icons have 44 px hit regions; list buttons have at least 48 px height. Selecting a place moves only the map camera, and preserves the player, order, delivery target and route.

The Sanjeevaiah Park national flag now uses equal horizontal stripes in a 3:2 rectangle with a centered navy Ashoka Chakra, 24 equally spaced spokes and visible geometry on both faces. The original horizontal cylinder was not a visible Chakra on the vertical cloth. Browser rendering exposed a face-winding error during development; it was repaired, and the regression now verifies outward normals for every spoke, rim and hub polygon on both faces. The flag's physical size, mast location and height remain inherited estimates. Digital colors are approximate sRGB render colors, not textile or BIS certification.

Charminar's four great arch openings now measure 11 m (20 m panel × template opening ratio 0.55), matching the Hyderabad district architecture description. Both the district and current Telangana Tourism pages independently describe 48.7 m minarets. The unsupported inherited upper tiers were therefore shortened from about 55.8 m to 48.7 m while retaining the 11 m arch opening and lower gallery. This follows published dimensions, not a new survey. Carvings, roof mosque detail and surveyed orientation remain under review.

Jagannath Temple now has a ribbed curving main tower, an eight-tier stepped pidha porch roof, separate open eight-spoke bronze Nilachakra finials and an east-facing front. The trust's own architecture page provides the 72 ft main tower/finial target and eight-spoke/east-facing descriptors; two independently dated original exterior photographs from 2017 and 2021 confirm the visible roof and finial forms. Plain red/yellow pennants remain, without invented marks; their poles project above the approximately 22 m stone/finial target. Sculptural carvings and auxiliary shrine placement remain approximate.

Central Gurudwara Saheb now has a raised entrance stair flight, stacked three-arch white pavilions and shallow gold domes, replacing the generic single tall onion dome. The source photograph was taken on 14 October 2025 and published by its photographer. Its flagcloth is furled: the plain inherited flag is retained pending a clear owner reference rather than inventing a Khanda or changing the color. Facade proportions and orientation remain approximate. Telangana Thalli's generic all-gold robed silhouette is replaced by a green sari/red blouse, raised right palm and left crop bundle visible in the government's official 9 December 2024 unveiling photographs. The existing approximate anchor/scale are retained, and exact face, jewellery, pedestal relief and measurements remain unverified. The retrieved statue image has a grey lower portion; it supports upper-body clothing/pose only, and no pedestal details were inferred from it. Companion government imagery confirms the illustrated pose.

Original source URLs, dates, photographers, licenses, hashes and visible observations are in [source-reviews.json](source-reviews.json). Source photographs were inspected in temporary local storage and are not packaged into the game.

`../landmark-audit.json` inventories all 191 entries, their model recipes, icon/category, bound/tile coverage, missing dimensions, inherited provenance and unresolved source/appearance review. **This is a metadata and geometry audit, not 191 photo-verified reconstructions. Four models have bounded photo-reviewed silhouette features; their full accuracy is still provisional.** The Gurdwara flag design, other generic temple flags, Secretariat lion-capital ornament and named-statue likenesses remain flagged for primary-image review. The Jagannath wheel has been corrected using owner documentation and original photos. No invented religious symbols, sacred text, national-emblem inscriptions or portrait details were added.
