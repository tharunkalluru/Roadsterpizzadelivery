3. Reconstruct the actual volumetric body and detailed head; native body UV bake, anatomical head graft and anatomical hands retain real geometry.

5. Export bounded self-contained compact GLBs with indexed geometry and JPEG89 textures. Retain original rest-height contracts: the loader validates before applying the seated Idle pose.
