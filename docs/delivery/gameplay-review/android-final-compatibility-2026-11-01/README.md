# Android emulator compatibility investigation — 7344, 8a4 and 87e9

This investigation began with the existing Roadster Pixel 7a test virtual device (API 37.1, `emulator-5554`) in Android Studio, with Chrome and the private device harness. The initial APK was the previously prepared `/tmp/roadster-private-device-final-20261101.apk`, SHA-256 `d23ba2f5f505e2ab13d81df9e82b7f6a3cbca0cf1d01e2a557ab0266686cdb0d`, corresponding to the earlier source fingerprint `734431db5999dc3291b5ae6d262ce401daf5b0c738b46ef92ce1e6bebceadc62`. This snapshot predates the later [reviewed rail-interlock fix](../train-turnout-interlock-fix-2026-11-01/source-model-review.json) and was [withheld for its turnout deadlock](../final-frozen-soak-2026-11-01/rejected-run-observations.json). The later builds and API35 comparison are recorded below; the current tested source fingerprint is `87e9f21b86ac8d14a27acfcdee7118d3b689741c98ba2c132a3f81a8ad98dbd6`.

1. In portrait Chrome, the game showed the blocking “Turn your phone sideways” prompt with “Enter Full Screen Mode”: [portrait screenshot](01-portrait-prompt-7344.png).
2. Tapping that button entered fullscreen and requested a landscape-oriented view. The Android Studio device frame was rotated with its Rotate Left control to view it normally. Play then reached a centered, readable Driving Tip with a “Got It” action: [landscape screenshot](02-landscape-tip-7344.png).
3. After dismissing the tip, the minimap and touch HUD appeared, but the 3D world remained a uniform dark background even after an additional 20 seconds: [screenshot](03-hud-no-world-7344.png). This does not demonstrate road rendering, driving, metro gameplay, touch interaction in the world, or frame rate.

The screenshots are original captures from Android Studio's device screenshot control. This virtual-device experiment is not a physical-phone test, and supplies no valid FPS, frame pacing, thermal, battery, or runtime-duration result. The later builds also failed to show the actual game world in the comparisons below. Final physical-phone validation remains outstanding; the earlier [physical reservation attempt](../physical-final-quota-2026-11-01/observations.json) allocated no device because free minutes were exhausted.

## Rendering investigation

While the blank world was visible, read-only emulator logcat repeatedly reported Chromium ANGLE `ProgramGL.cpp:836 (checkLinkStatus): Program link or binary loading failed with no info log`. The logs alone do not establish whether a game shader, renderer state, or the emulator backend caused the blank world; no matching JavaScript exception was present in the filtered Chromium log.

An independent, temporary one-shot diagnostic in emulator Chrome reported its unmasked renderer as Android Emulator OpenGL ES Translator, ANGLE over Vulkan SwiftShader. Raw WebGL and WebGL 2, simple Three.js basic and high-rendering checks, and a separate case using the game's engine each reported passing colored pixels in the [corrected run](07-corrected-graphics-check.png). In the earlier [summary](05-renderer-diagnostic.png) and [detail](06-engine-diagnostic-detail.png), the game-engine case incorrectly reported failure because its fixed pixel sample missed the resized framebuffer center; the diagnostic was corrected and rerun. These narrow synthetic checks do not establish that the actual game world is rendering or identify the cause of the blank game screen.

## Later build and comparison

The rail-fix build (`8a4f4cd41306ef6b1d4061e079d9e52447c5f6bb72183e25a4646353b3e1562c`) also showed a blank world on this emulator both after [portrait-to-landscape](12-railfix-portrait-transition-high.png) and on a [fresh landscape launch](13-railfix-landscape-start-high.png). Its on-screen diagnostic reported a clear paint gate, a visible and focused document, no blockers, hundreds of render calls, and a nonzero canvas size. The regular mobile graphics option in a separate normal-start scenario showed the same blank-world symptom; the two quality levels were not compared in the same scenario.

The later render-diagnostic build (`87e9f21b86ac8d14a27acfcdee7118d3b689741c98ba2c132a3f81a8ad98dbd6`) still showed a blank world when its QA control hid the HUD. Its [export](09-renderdiag-export.json) records visible, opaque `#game` and canvas elements; a finite camera and projection; no lost context; and no paint blockers. A normal-renderer [canvas PNG](08-renderdiag-canvas.png) was transparent at all 2,204 sampled grid points, but the synthetic control below limits the interpretation of that PNG.

The published game was opened on the same emulator and a real order was accepted through its visible slider; after the tip was dismissed, its [HUD was also visible over a blank world](14-published-control.png). This reproduces the symptom on the published build as well and does not establish a new fourteen-scene regression or a completed delivery.

A separate fullscreen continuous-rendering control using the engine reported a green opaque `readPixels` sample and no GL or collected shader errors, with scissor testing off before and after warmup and the default framebuffer bound. Its [lifecycle export](11-lifecycle-control.json) notes `preserveDrawingBuffer: false`; the saved [canvas PNG](10-lifecycle-control.png) appeared transparent despite that colored readback. Consequently, transparent canvas-to-PNG output alone does not prove the renderer drew transparent pixels. The cause of the visible blank world on this emulator remains unresolved.

An additional Studio-owned cold boot used the existing Android 15/API 35 virtual device, temporarily switching its graphics setting from Automatic to Hardware. The emulator's SurfaceFlinger reported Android Emulator OpenGL ES Translator (Apple M4), OpenGL ES 3.0 (4.1 Metal - 91.7). The same `87e9...` diagnostic APK's assisted Metro scenario again displayed a [HUD over a blank world](15-api35-hardware-renderdiag.png), with a clear paint gate, no reported blockers, a finite camera, and an available graphics context. This comparison is still an emulator test, uses the Mac's GPU, and does not establish physical-phone performance or the root cause. The emulator was stopped and its graphics setting restored to Automatic afterward.
