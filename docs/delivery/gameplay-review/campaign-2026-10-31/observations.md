Root also clears its old notice when a successful throw begins.

Root is retaining completed scene scenery separately from the interaction/pickup lock. New local `sceneryPending` keeps properties/actors eligible within850m; exit rules remain independent.

Ten preparation tests cover delayed/failed next assets, current failures, four-person metro priority, stale callbacks and retained assets.
