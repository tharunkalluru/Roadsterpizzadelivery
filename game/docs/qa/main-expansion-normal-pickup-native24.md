The frozen24 native Chrome campaign used ordinary on-screen driving controls
with full-campaign staging disabled. Initial jump attempts missed; the player
steered toward the live carrier, waited through the ordinary cooldown and
collected the slice during another moving jump. No Prepare, teleport or pickup
assistance was used.

The pickup removed the offer slice and showed the compact box on the trunk.
The road route and minimap guidance appeared, with 2.2 km remaining and “Follow
the road”. The box did not occupy the forward view. Pause remained available.

The immutable build identity, two native screenshots and exact observation are
in `evidence/main-expansion/normal-pickup-native24/`. This is pickup/cargo and
limited control evidence. It is not a completed delivery, final road-profile
approval, character-meal approval, physical-phone measurement or a 30–40 minute
gameplay benchmark. `qaTouch=1` exercises the responsive control layout on the
desktop; it does not turn the computer into a phone.
