Traffic meshes now pitch to the finite reachable road beneath their nose and tail. Graph position/height, heading, road progress, speed, clocks, routing and avoidance remain unchanged. The visual root retains its previous `pathY + .02` height. The same method runs when a vehicle spawns and after ordinary movement; missing pavement or a separate overhead layer supplies no tilt.

The regression uses the actual merged bus, auto and sedan meshes against independently evaluated emitted Float32 asphalt. Before this change, a yaw-only bus on a 10% plane had 338.6 mm tyre penetration and 262.1 mm visible body penetration; the auto had 91.3 mm tyre penetration and the sedan 54.2 mm body penetration. The corrected rising/falling 7.5% and 10% planes pass at spawn and throughout normal 30/60 Hz movement in both directions. The largest tyre-height change from the existing flat mesh is 0.063 mm; minimum non-tread body clearance is 87.8 mm. The original polygonal tyre/visual lift baseline is retained, not presented as exact tyre contact.

Seven focused groups pass, including finite-pavement/overhead/flat controls, exact paired kinematic/root-position/yaw/clock parity, and actual pitched-bus roof queries at its front, middle and rear. The old collision interval missed a visible high-end roof at 23.654 m because it stopped at 23.300 m. Player hit and push-out queries now clip the existing nominal body box to the player's local longitudinal footprint after pitch; a jump over the lower end does not inherit the higher roof. The existing flat box and its original allowance above lower roof panels are retained exactly. Thirteen existing approach groups, sixteen traffic regression groups, seven bus execution groups, eighteen bus/service/text groups, seven jump groups and twelve camera-visibility groups pass. Source hashes and before/after logs are retained in `evidence/main-expansion/traffic-road-posture-source-hold.json`.

The paired warmed desktop CPU sample uses the same 55 actual traffic vehicles on 1500 two-metre changing-grade road spans, without a geometry oracle inside timing. Median normal update cost was 3.908 ms/frame with the helper and 3.578 ms/frame without; paired median delta 0.3166 ms/frame, or 5.756 µs/vehicle. The eight paired observations range from 0.1767 to 0.5249 ms/frame. This is local Node CPU evidence, not mobile FPS or device acceptance. The reusable probe and full observations are under `evidence/main-expansion/traffic-road-posture-cpu.*`.

This source hold does not close the final road gate. Complete vehicle geometry must still be checked on the actual final changing-grade prepared ramps/aprons and curve transitions. In particular, curve endpoint interpolation can differ from the final nonlinear road profile; constant-plane evidence alone does not establish that case.
