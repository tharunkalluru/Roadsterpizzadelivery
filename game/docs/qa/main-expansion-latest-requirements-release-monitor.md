## New performance finding

`actor-performance.ts` performs `receivedCartonDepth()` in `receivePizza()`.
That function traverses the complete posed costume geometry, computes skinned
vertices and then runs hand-support calibration. The call occurs at the actual
handoff, after the earlier hidden-prop GPU preparation. In an exact-geometry,
texture-stripped Node microbenchmark on this computer, that synchronous call
took 5.68–14.00ms across the eleven eligible recipients; most took 10–12ms.
Installation itself took 8.83–21.90ms, measured separately after parsing.

The benchmark source, bundle and raw output are preserved at
`/tmp/roadster-latest-meal-cost.ts`, `.mjs` and `.log`. These are one-run CPU
samples while other work was active, not browser frame-time measurements or
phone FPS. Nevertheless, the whole-mesh scan is concrete work on the handoff
frame. Move/precompute the exact receiving-pose depth/support work into the
existing instance preparation window, or establish through a browser trace
that the handoff frame stays within budget. Preserve the accepted v4 contact
poses and the idle appearance; do not replace accurate support with estimates.

## Source coverage and limits

- The arrival gate rejects flight, descent, jump, glide, motion, unsupported
  tyres and the wrong deck; it resets on relocation/visibility and requires a
  continuous settled interval. Rail exceptions retain real manual coach/rake
  contact. No additional functional gate defect was confirmed in this review.
- Trunk carton visibility is connected to assignment, actual transfer,
  completion, restart and teardown; offered slices remain separate. Cached
  jump bounds invalidate on a visibility change rather than every frame.
- The density increase is bounded and retains existing motion clocks. Its
  higher count still needs final-build browser frame-pacing evidence; this
  review cannot infer that a mid-range GPU meets 60 FPS.
- Lip data is explicitly bound to the exact asset and reviewed anatomical
  region, with a silent normalized envelope for future audio. Actual audio
  synchronization has deliberately not been implemented.
- Prop/sparse-face resources are instance-owned and participate in scene
  teardown. The restaurant/next-level cleanup retains only a bounded number
  of completed scene instances.

Root owns the remaining native meal presentation, new truck reservation and
final integrated play gates. The separate junction/source/profile/cache stage
is still inactive and unaccepted. Earlier frozen20 scene and compatibility
checks do not approve those later road candidates or the new density cost.
