# Integrated gameplay checks

These are development-scenario checks in the existing game, using its real controls, world, physics, UI and delivery actions. QA buttons stage an order/approach or a real train landing; they do not call completion, advance calls, or perform the drop. They are not natural pickup-to-delivery playthroughs or physical-phone tests.

## Observed

- **Truck, desktop, Low graphics:** staged behind the actual moving ramp, held W to drive inside, used Space to brake and S to adjust position, reached the grounded interior handoff state, pressed F and observed the Level1/14 completion/cart. This exposed the interior navigation/early cleanup issues; source fixes and production-physics exit tests were added afterwards.
- **Ram, desktop, Low graphics:** landed through normal descent on rear coach7 of the actual Vande Bharat shuttle, drove with W and braked with Space across coaches7,6,5,4,3,2. The live contact readout recorded every coach, all8 remained visible, and the car stayed at the physical roof height. At1m from the marker the action became ready; F completed Level2/14.
- **Devara, 914×412 landscape touch layout, Low graphics:** staged the car at the mapped water approach, tapped Dive, waited for actual depth−6m, tapped Place Pizza, waited for actual surfacing, then observed Level3/14 completion. QA controls were removed to inspect unobstructed touch controls and the tableau. This found the initial skeleton/car overlap; the tableau now frames six metres ahead of the arrival heading and has a four-heading regression test.
- **Arundhati, 914×412 landscape touch layout, Low graphics:** watched incremental scene preparation finish at the actual new ground anchor, staged at the gate, removed QA controls, tapped Deliver and observed Level5/14 completion. Ground guidance correctly said Approach marker rather than requiring flight. The Arundhati model remains withheld.
- **Nani, 914×412 landscape touch layout, Low graphics:** prepared the real stadium scene, landed at the existing turf height (car centre approximately0.6m), tapped Drop during the live break-in-play window, observed the final restaurant opening and returned to free roam on the original field. No duplicate floating pitch/stands were added. This isolated scenario pre-completes the previous thirteen levels; it does not establish a complete campaign playthrough.

Screenshots record the states named in their filenames. `truck-delivery-desktop-draft.png` is the earlier truck completion, not proof of the later exit-retention fix. `train-six-coach-traverse.png` contains the explicit coach contact history. `underwater-touch-depth.png` deliberately shows no Devara actor because his appearance is withheld by the release gate.

This original mechanics snapshot was recorded before independent character approvals. For current per-character approval states and later named-model checks, read [the current campaign build report](../fourteen-scene-build.md). These checks validate mechanics/layout only, not likeness, complete cinematic acceptance, real-phone FPS, heating, battery life or the entire fourteen-level journey. Broader final campaign/device playtesting must follow approved art integration.
