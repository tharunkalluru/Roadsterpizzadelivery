# Street and frontage reconstruction record

The scope is **all 10,276 road edges (approximately 654 km), both sides**, including service streets and boundary edges. It is not a claim that these roads have already been visually surveyed. `public/research/street-survey.json` is the authoritative authored layer. Base world JSON and its integrity hashes are not rewritten by this layer.

## Record evidence before placement

1. Open a road in Chrome using the survey atlas. Inspect both directions and both sides, then record the visible capture month, panorama/source URL, viewing coordinates and heading. A capture point is a camera location, not a shop entrance.
2. Add a `sources` entry. Record ambiguous dates as unknown (`null`) and keep imagery `reference_only` unless an explicit open license permits asset reuse. Do not download Google imagery into textures or redistribute it.
3. Record a visible shop as an `observations` entry with `placementStatus: "pending"` until its street side, actual building/floor, facade endpoints, entrance and neighbour sequence are established. A search-result address or nearest listing does not prove placement.
4. Author a `storefrontOverrides` entry with explicit `placementStatus`: use `provisional` for researched reconstructions with incomplete geometry/floor proof, and `verified` only when every required dimension is supported. Include the existing world road ID, chunk ID, building ID (or observed kiosk), local facade/entrance geometry, floor, frontage width, road arclength and proof source IDs for identity, position, side, floor, facade and neighbours. A provisional record may have empty unproven dimension arrays; its notes must identify estimates. `appearance` carries independently authored facade/sign colours and dimensions. Record the capture date and review date separately.
5. Identify displaced listing IDs, place IDs or nearby same-brand aliases. Automatic alias suppression is limited to the same road and side within 80 m by default, rather than removing other branches throughout Hyderabad.
6. Add bounded `reviewedRanges` to the appropriate road side. Order `storefrontIds` by arclength in the stored road points direction, even if imagery was inspected in the opposite direction. `allFrontagesRecorded: true` records an end-to-end **visual inventory** of visible units, floors and non-shop frontage, supported by endpoint/intermediate and reverse-angle views. Include unreadable, hidden or uncertain operators as explicit unresolved observations; this does not imply exact reconstruction. Keep range `completeness: "partial"` and side `status: "partial"` while facade geometry remains provisional. Only use `completeness: "complete"` when every positioned frontage has verified geometry evidence, and mark a whole side `verified` only when those complete ranges cover its length without gaps. A few observed shops do not verify a street.
7. Run `npm run survey:validate`, then `npm run survey:network`. Commit source and geometry evidence together. `npm run survey:check` verifies reproducibility without changing files.

## Files and API

- `public/research/street-survey.json`: versioned source records, every road's review status, observations, provisional/verified storefront overrides, evidence-backed suppressions and corrections.
- `public/research/street-survey-summary.json`: reproducible, separately labelled observation windows, visual inventory and strict verified counts/lengths. Percentages use both sides of every road as denominator; overlapping ranges are merged. `visuallyInventoriedSides` counts only complete visual coverage of a whole canonical side; bounded smaller inventories still add their actual length.
- `public/research/survey-network.json`: compact full-network research inventory with road name, length, midpoint coordinates, forward bearing and each side's status. Geometry remains in the canonical core package.
- `src/world/streetSurvey.ts`: validates data in browser and scripts; provides `StreetSurvey.segment`, `summary`, immutable `applyChunk` and `applyCoreCorrections`.
- `public/research/survey-work-queue.json`: ordered citywide inventory covering every road and both sides once, in connected graph blocks; nearest-station corridor labels are work regions, not surveyed streets.
- `src/world/streetSurveyBatch.ts` and `scripts/survey-import-batch.mjs`: validated immutable merge, SHA-256 receipts, conflict guards and evidence-backed retractions for [reviewed JSON block batches](batches/README.md).

Road left/right is relative to its stored polyline, not a player's current direction. X points east; Z points south, matching the canonical schema. Survey widths, floor numbers and facade positions must not be generated from category templates and called verified.

Floor `0` means ground tenancy; negative integers denote basement levels. `appearance.frontBaseM` separately sets the authored exposed-front elevation, so a semi-basement can be drawn at road level without mislabelling its tenancy. `frontInsetM`, `signOutsetM` and `signWidthM` describe authored facade offsets and sign dimensions; they do not turn estimated geometry into measured evidence.

A `profile.signageOnly` overlay adds observed secondary boards while preserving existing building architecture and access. Its canonical sign faces have empty floor arrays and cannot contain access, walls, glazing, setbacks or other architecture. It does not create a storefront/tenant or extend reviewed-road coverage. The revision 33 batch adds the brown-block Samsung repair/accessories wording, secondary NOTARY STAMP PAPERS and MOUNIKA XEROX near the MedPlus stair, using existing April 2026 observations; exact panel positions, dimensions and uncertain colours remain estimates.

Reusable profiles can describe photographed arched openings and balustrades as well as glazing, piers and floor bands. A sourced `signPanels` advertisement belongs to the profile and does not add a storefront or tenant. Optional normalized `at` positions a board within its source face; the full width must stay within that face. `frontStyle: "open_bay"` preserves visibly open shop fronts, while `observedDisplay` and `displaySide` record photographed stock and its road-view side; neither business category nor listing name can supply that evidence. Preserve visible historical sale dates in its text and notes. Neither an advertisement nor a repeated roof/ground business board establishes another independent premises.

`building_profile` corrections can restore a photographed building that was wrongly omitted by an estimated road-clearance conflict. `retainPresence` needs dated street-level evidence; estimated `heightM`/`levels` must keep `dimensionStatus: "provisional"`. This does not certify the adjacent road width. `building_height` and `road_width` corrections default to provisional; an explicit verified dimension requires field-survey evidence. `traffic_signal` corrections establish sourced junction locations while game-authored timings and junction geometry remain provisional.
