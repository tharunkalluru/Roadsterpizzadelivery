# Hyderabad Metro Delivery (working title "Metro Express")

> Handoff snapshot: validated survey revision **102**, 708 sources, 551 observations and 169 provisional storefronts. Strict verification remains zero. See [root STATUS](../STATUS.md) and the current root ASSIGNMENTS; the revision-specific narrative below is historical and does not activate work.

A single-player Three.js pizza-delivery driving game on Hyderabad roads around the Red, Blue and Green metro corridors. Accept an order, drive to a food-business pickup, stop and **explicitly collect the pizza**, then drive to the destination and **explicitly hand it over**. Driving earns short flight boosts for the Roadster-inspired car.

**Version 0.2.0 / survey revision 62 is an initial rebuild, with the full-map street reconstruction still unfinished.** The playable listing-based network exists across all three operating metro corridors. Exact street-side businesses, facades, road dimensions and building heights have only begun to receive imagery review; do not treat the existing generated city as a fully reconstructed Hyderabad.

## Play

- **Touch:** steer with the left pad. Use ▲ to accelerate and ▼ to brake; keep holding ▼ when stopped to reverse. FLY/LAND toggles a flight request. At a pickup/customer marker, stop and tap **COLLECT PIZZA** or **HAND OVER**. Map and pause buttons are on the HUD.
- **Keyboard:** W/↑ accelerate; S/↓ brake/reverse; A/D or ←/→ steer; Space/F fly/land; **E collect/hand over**; P/Esc pause; M map; H horn.
- Releasing road controls slows and holds the car. There is no automatic cruising.
- While stopped, drag the empty scene to look around the car. Drag down to look up at upper-floor shops; driving gently restores the chase view.
- Accepting a contract starts an approach journey to its pickup with the cargo unloaded. Enter the correct road-level stop area, face the valid approach, stop and press E/the action button. Remain stopped for approximately one second to complete collection or handover. Merely passing a marker does not complete it.
- Food categories determine pickup eligibility; pizza outlets are preferred where compatible routes exist. Business identities and entrances inherited from the listing base remain provisional.
- Start with **2.5 seconds of boost**. Every **10 qualifying road-driving seconds earns 2.5 seconds** of flight charge, accumulating up to the contract's capacity. The 20/25/30-second contract values are storage capacities, not a fixed lifetime allowance. Idle, paused, airborne, recovery, off-carriageway and collision time do not recharge it. Qualifying road speed is at least 2 m/s.
- Launch and descent consume charge; bounded protected descent assists landing after charge exhaustion and cannot be used for cruising or another launch. Current fictional game speed caps are 120 km/h on the road and 150 km/h in the air.

Five contract levels progress from neighbourhood trips to cross-city deliveries. Completing a delivery in a level unlocks the next. Debug runs do not save records.

## Street atlas and map

Choose **Explore Hyderabad** from the main menu. Search roads, filter by station area or review progress, select a segment, open its Street View reference, and use **Drive this street** to inspect it in the game. Every stored road edge has separate left/right review records. Directions are relative to the road's stored polyline, not the player's current heading.

The in-game map has traffic, street and research layers. Traffic colours reflect the active local simulation. Roads outside the simulated traffic area are not claimed to have measured live congestion; this is not a connection to Google Maps traffic.

The map now fetches visible building footprints independently of the driving scene, with a bounded 120-tile cache. You can inspect distant streets before driving there. The citywide index contains **37,583 named source listing points within the playable boundary**. Map and search apply researched replacements and explicit suppressions before showing the remaining source points as grey markers at their original coordinates. They are orientation aids, not verified tenants or entrances; the complete raw archive is preserved. Connected-block navigation and left/right research links cover all **1,913 blocks**; generated links do not prove that imagery exists.

## World data and current reconstruction

The default `public/world/hyd-metro/v2/` package contains **10,276 road edges, approximately 654 km of road, 80,076 buildings, 19,507 provisional business frontages and 57 metro stations** around the three metro lines. It is the playable corridor network, not every street in metropolitan Hyderabad. Overture Places supplies business names and point locations; a nearest-building attachment does not establish the correct facade, floor, entrance or neighbouring shop order. Much of the building height and road width data is inferred. The original `hyd-uppal-tarnaka/v1/` package remains available with `?v1`.

A separate integrity-checked `source-accuracy.json` overlay now audits **every building and original frontage**. Before manual survey corrections, height precedence is **28 explicit source heights → 231 source floor-derived heights → 50,286 satellite height estimates → 29,531 disclosed class placeholders**. The satellite estimates use the 2023 [Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal dataset](https://sites.research.google/gr/open-buildings/temporal/) under CC BY 4.0, derived from Copernicus Sentinel-2 data processed by ESA. Low-presence pixels are masked; robust footprint medians retain sample counts, spread and usable coverage. Effective resolution is 4 m, roof alignment can drift, and measured height accuracy in Hyderabad has not been established. Researched profiles override these estimates. Source facade colours, material tags, floor counts and the sole raised minimum-height record are retained where present.

## Architecture and diagnostics

`src/app` manages lifecycle and streaming; `src/core` runs the fixed 60 Hz simulation; `src/player` handles driving, flight and charge accounting; `src/world` provides routing, collision, loading and survey overlays. `src/traffic`, `src/missions`, `src/ui`, `src/art`, `src/audio`, `src/persistence` and `src/debug` provide the remaining game systems. `scripts/` contains data import, research, food-index and validation tools.

Append `?debug` for diagnostics, `?debug&manualclock` for test stepping, or `?debug&autopilot` to watch the reference driving agent. Version 0.2.0 checkpoints reflect the new pickup and rechargeable-boost lifecycle.

## Remaining limitations

- Full street-by-street and shop-by-shop reconstruction is **unfinished**. The majority of the playable map remains listing-based and unreviewed; correct business names do not imply correct frontage placement.
- No complete measured building-height, street-width, station-interior, traffic-signal phase or curb inventory exists. There is no metric terrain/DEM layer: most roads are flat placeholders, with authored bridge/tunnel elevations and ramps. Reconstructed architecture and traffic remain game interpretations with disclosed source gaps.
- Traffic and metro activity run in the game simulation; their density and headways are not measured live Hyderabad operations.
- Whiteout remains a visual-quality reference; equivalent graphics and complete matched-view comparisons are not claimed.
- Performance has not been established on physical Android/iPhone devices. Night/rain scenarios use game-authored atmosphere; their appearance and performance on physical phones remain unmeasured.

## Lossless runtime-data setup

The complete source-accuracy layer is tracked as `game/data/archives/source-accuracy.json.gz` (about 2 MB), with exact byte count and SHA-256 in `game/config/runtime-data-archives.json`. `npm ci` restores `game/public/world/hyd-metro/v2/source-accuracy.json` automatically; build/dev/test and survey scripts also run the restore preflight. If install scripts are disabled, run `cd game && npm run setup:data` explicitly. Decompression uses only Node built-ins, performs no network access, and must reproduce the full original **19,856,474 bytes**, SHA-256 `53ffc82503447ec72846894343e1562c9f5f33456514bfc90aadea3103111e05`. World data, manifests and runtime hashes are unchanged. The restored JSON is ignored by Git; do not commit it.

Only the single integrator may update this generated layer: preserve/review the new JSON, run `npm run archive:data`, and commit the archive plus its checksum manifest and related world bindings together. Setup refuses to overwrite an existing divergent output. Keep source/licence attribution with the compressed data. No reference imagery, records or precision were removed.
