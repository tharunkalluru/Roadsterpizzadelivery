# CONTENT_GAPS: what the imported world does not establish

## Current state — survey revision 47, 2026-10-18

The default game now covers the selected Red/Blue/Green corridor network, with source overlays and limited dated Chrome research. It is **not a complete reconstruction of Hyderabad streets**. The current scope is 10,276 road edges / 654.21 km, 20,552 sides, 80,076 original building outlines, 19,507 original front records and 57 stations. Source names and imagery-informed estimates do not establish exact frontage placement or measured dimensions.

At revision 47 there are **159 provisional front/sign records on 42 profiles, 207 sources and 273 observations**. 45 sides have partial review windows totalling 3,457.79 m; visual inventories cover 1,552.75 m / 0.119% of total side length. 19 canonical sides have a complete visual inventory, with unknown operators and dimensions still explicit. **Strictly verified sides and storefront placements remain zero.** The queue covers every road in 1,913 connected blocks, but queue coverage is not surveyed coverage.

The frozen archive flags 5,502 building outlines. Four manual presence overrides leave 5,498 omitted before recovery. Safe provisional parts restore 5,485 of those parents; **13 bodies remain unresolved and omitted**. Forty-nine extra parts from split outlines produce 80,125 component records / 80,112 visible masses, not extra independent buildings. Road/carriageway estimates were reconciled to retain safe masses; they were not measured or declared geographically exact.

Nine of 11 source station envelopes now inform horizontal dimensions; Nagole/MGBS require custom layouts and eight other records are unmatched. Seven entrance points remain provisional same-site associations. Four basement openings now have real lower floors/treads, walls, ceilings, shared terrain holes and physical support, but their 0.66 m drop is an explicit estimate. Xiaomi's wider basement forecourt/descent remains a photographed gap without invented excavation; only its fitted shared raised landing/two ascent flights are modeled. Station vertical dimensions and full interiors remain authored. These changes close implementation gaps without closing the measurement/reconstruction gaps below.

[Source accuracy](data/research/source-accuracy.md), [street survey](data/research/street-survey.md) and the generated survey summaries are the current references. No field measurement is claimed. The v1 C1–C30 record below is retained as historical acquisition history; statements such as "no imagery review" or "no Red/Green roads" apply to that old package only. Current v2 gaps follow after it.
